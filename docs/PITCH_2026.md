# Zim Safe Minds — pitch room rehearsal

## 90-second founder pitch

> In Zimbabwe, getting mental-health support can begin with a difficult decision: telling someone you need help. For some young adults, stigma, cost and uncertainty about who to trust can stop that first step.
>
> We already have organizations doing valuable work, including Friendship Bench. The gap we want to test isn't the absence of care — it is the difficult journey **from needing help to reaching it**.
>
> Zim Safe Minds is a private, low-data, youth-designed care navigator. A young person can try an optional supportive self-expression demo, choose whether they prefer online or face-to-face support, select an affordability and language preference, and explore credible public services. Later, with qualified partners and strict consent, they'll be able to request an introduction to verified professionals without automatically sharing intimate chat messages.
>
> We have already built the interactive prototype. It isn't an AI therapist, and we won't pretend that it is. The proposed innovation is a safe bridge to real people, not technology replacing care.
>
> With the US$5,000 seed grant, we'll spend twelve months validating the unmet need, co-designing the safety model with clinicians, verifying partners, and running a small consenting-adult pilot. We'll judge success by whether young people can actually reach trustworthy support, not by how long they chat with a bot.
>
> Zim Safe Minds. A safe space to talk. A path to real support.

## Three-minute demo itinerary

1. **0:00–0:25 — root cause:** explain stigma and youth service access using Category 1 wording, not generic 'mental health is bad'.
2. **0:25–1:00 — start:** show the homepage and consent explanation, then demonstrate one scripted supportive exchange. State unequivocally it is a prototype, not clinical AI.
3. **1:00–1:45 — care navigation:** move from chat to 'Care navigator'; choose online, free, and Shona; show instant preference-based summary, no sensitive text transfer.
4. **1:45–2:10 — credible options:** click through to the existing Friendship Bench website and explain it is a public independent provider *not a project partnership*. Professional directory is planned and not yet live.
5. **2:10–2:35 — operational reality:** show timeline, partner verification and clinical safety gate, US$5,000 allocation.
6. **2:35–3:00 — impact:** 50-person proposed pilot, care contact metrics, scalability via partnerships; close with tagline.

## Difficult judge questions and grounded answers

**'Is this simply ChatGPT for therapy?'** No. There is no production AI integration or therapeutic service. The current demo uses scripted prompts. The proposed impact is an opt-in path to verified support with accountable service-access measures.

**'Friendship Bench already uses WhatsApp; why do we need you?'** Friendship Bench is doing essential work and might be a collaborator if they independently opt in. We aim to test the referral-navigation *gap* across services; we will not replace its counseling model. We will stop or pivot if evidence shows the gap doesn't exist.

**'Can US$5,000 build a medical-grade AI service?'** No, and that isn't the pilot scope. The budget buys research, safety governance, a low-data care-navigation prototype, partner verification and a small evaluation. Broad clinical AI is a separately gated future possibility.

**'What prevents an unsafe referral?'** Provider checks, user consent, human-operated protocols, visible urgent-help guidance, no automatic transcript sharing, limited scope and launch gates. Safety incidents and failures are pilot measures.

**'How do you earn revenue without exploiting vulnerable users?'** Explore institutional contracts for service navigation after proving utility; core navigation stays free and sensitive data is never sold. Financial viability is a hypothesis to test, not an existing revenue stream.

**'Who is the team?'** Answer only with actual eligible team members and roles. The official scheme encourages teams of 2–5 and prioritizes 40% female participation; do not claim team composition you haven't formed.

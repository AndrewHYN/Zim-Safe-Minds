## 90-second opening, ready to rehearse

> Imagine a young Zimbabwean who is struggling, but doesn't know who to talk to. They might be worried about judgment, uncertain about fees or qualifications, or simply unable to find the words to make a first appointment.
>
> **Zim Safe Minds** is designed for that difficult first step. We are building a welcoming, privacy-conscious pathway that helps a young person move from private reflection to trustworthy human support.
>
> Our working prototype has three sections. **Talk** offers optional guided reflection and simple grounding prompts. Today the conversation is a clearly labelled scripted demo; live AI is part of our future, clinically reviewed roadmap. **Learn** offers original mental-health awareness articles linked to independent sources. **Connect** helps users choose preferences and prepare their own first message, without automatically sharing what they said in chat.
>
> We know valuable services such as Friendship Bench already exist. We are **not** trying to replace them. Our hypothesis is that making the very first contact easier can help young people reach the care that already exists.
>
> We are seeking **US$5,000** to run an ethical 12-month pilot: listen to youth, consult mental-health professionals, verify services, improve low-data accessibility and evaluate real first contacts. Our success measure is not time spent chatting with an AI. It is **whether someone can take a safe first step toward real human care**.
>
> Zim Safe Minds: **a safe space to talk, a path to real support**.

## Questions for judges

**Is the AI really built?** The user-facing interaction is implemented, but current responses are scripted and clearly labelled as such. We intend to integrate a well-evaluated model only following safeguards, privacy review and clinical oversight. We will not claim therapeutic capabilities.

**Are providers available to book?** There are public independent support links and a user-controlled inquiry preview. Real credential-checked provider bookings are future work and require independent partner verification and user consent.

**What is novel compared with Friendship Bench?** Friendship Bench already delivers meaningful services. We hypothesize a separate, low-pressure first-contact bridge reduces uncertainty across the journey to care. We will validate this with youth and professionals; pivot if evidence disagrees.

**How will you evaluate the solution?** Count consented users who discover a relevant service and successfully make a first human contact, record unsuccessful journeys and accessibility issues, assess staff workload/safety incidents. No clinical mental-health improvement will be inferred from session duration.

**Will $5,000 build an AI therapist?** No. This budget funds a scoped 12-month research, governance and limited-pilot program, not a medical-grade nationwide AI service.

**How big is your team?** Currently forming a team of four, two men and two women. Specific people and the Lead are selected by the group, not fabricated in this public repo. Andrew Hama Mutamiri is the Technical Lead. Update if actual membership changes.

# Zim Safe Minds — Team Kickoff & Role Choices

**For the four-person group preparing for the Zimbabwe Youth Innovation HackLab 2026.**  
**Deadline: 20 October 2026 at 23:59 CAT | Category 1 only**

Welcome! Please start with the [README](../README.md), then [live demo](https://andrewhyn.github.io/Zim-Safe-Minds/) and [internal pitch](PITCH_TO_TEAM.md). This is a chance to co-design the project; roles are *suggestions*, not assignments.

## 1. Where we are

- Four prospective team members (two women, two men) — 50% female membership if all join. The published priority threshold is 40%; **adding a fifth female member is optional**, not required to meet that percentage.
- The official team size is 2–5; everyone must be 18–35 and ordinarily resident in Zimbabwe. Only the chosen **Team Lead** is the application's primary contact.
- **Andrew Hama Mutamiri is developing the prototype and will act as Technical Lead, not Team Lead.**
- The actual Team Lead and the other individual responsibilities have **not been assigned**.
- A solo application draft exists, but it must be adapted for a team once everyone has agreed; **no application submitted**.
- Real clinical providers or researchers have **not** agreed to partner. We cannot imply otherwise.

## 2. Role menu — pick by interest, not gender

| Role | A good fit if you like... | Deliverable before HackLab deadline | Ongoing pilot focus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Team Lead / Pitch Coordinator** | Planning, speaking, organizing people | Verify all team details, submit one application, coordinate 3-minute demo | Reporting, milestones, partnerships and pitch |
| **Technical Lead / Developer** | Building software, UI, testing | Stable live demo and rehearsed technical explanation | Platform, accessibility, data security, responsible AI |
| **Youth Research / Wellbeing Liaison** | Listening, non-clinical research, stakeholder contact | Plan and, if feasible, complete small consented youth interviews; document anonymous themes | User research, professional consultation, safeguards |
| **Community / Inclusion / UX** | Accessibility, language, public communication | Low-end phone walkthrough, review gender/disability access, rewrite unclear text | Recruitment, inclusive design, outreach |
| **Operations / Monitoring & Evaluation** | Tracking tasks, budgeting and measurement | Review $5,000 plan for realism, scorecard/evidence log | Records, grant reporting and sustainability |

**How this works with four:** operations can be shared with Team Lead or another willing member, or other roles combined. If a fifth person joins, consider whether they meaningfully fill a gap rather than adding them simply for numbers. No role requires someone to reveal their gender publicly.

## 3. Meeting agenda (35 minutes)

| Time | Activity | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 min | Give the one-minute vision pitch | Everyone understands the root cause |
| 5–13 min | Live product walkthrough | Everyone has seen the scripted chat, grounding and care navigator |
| 13–21 min | Discussion: how is this better than or complementary to current services? | Record strongest concern and how to test it |
| 21–29 min | Voluntary role choices and Team Lead selection | Agree owners and decision rights |
| 29–35 min | Deadline plan and evidence assignments | Commit to real tasks, not assumptions |

### Questions to ask each teammate

1. Would a young Zimbabwean find this more approachable than visiting a clinic straight away?
2. What part feels confusing, impersonal, untrustworthy or too much like an AI demo?
3. Would you use the care navigator **without** using the AI chat? Why?
4. What would make the support pathway safe and credible?
5. What is one realistic improvement we should make **before** the deadline?

## 4. Who decides what?

- **Team Lead:** coordinates the group, is the official contact and submits only after member approval.
- **Technical Lead:** decides whether a proposed feature is safe and feasible to demo; checks test/build/deploy quality.
- **Research & Inclusion owners:** flag unclear evidence, unverified partners, unrepresentative claims, accessibility and informed-consent problems.
- **All team members:** review final application wording and agree before the Team Lead submits. Major public promises, clinical claims and budget commitments need a team decision.
- Where people disagree, record the concern and choose the smallest safe test; don't invent proof.

## 5. Product demo checklist

- [ ] Home page explains the stigma/help-seeking barrier clearly
- [ ] Guided chat is described as a **scripted** preview today; real AI is a later feature
- [ ] A user can skip AI and navigate directly to human-support information
- [ ] Care preferences and the copy-only first-contact note work
- [ ] No clinical provider/partnership is invented
- [ ] No personal mental-health disclosures are entered during demo
- [ ] Show both medical Daylight and Afterglow themes on mobile
- [ ] Build, tests and latest GitHub Pages deploy checked

## 6. Short-term priorities

**Before 20 October:** agree on a legitimate Team Lead, verify ages/residency, get real member consent, prepare a short pitch, review the existing application, conduct small non-clinical user discovery if possible and submit **one** form before the deadline. Candidate user interviews should ask about how people *find help*, not diagnoses, suicidal thoughts or medical histories.

**After the deadline / during a funded pilot:** co-design with qualified clinical advisers, safeguard sensitive data, independently verify providers, assess inclusive UX and test actual first-contact outcomes. **Do not launch AI therapy or real referrals as a rushed competition feature.**

# Zimbabwe Youth Innovation HackLab 2026 — Zim Safe Minds

**Proposed category:** Holistic Youth Health / Mental Wellbeing (confirm exact category wording and root cause in official call before applying).
**Applicant concept:** Youth-owned, early prototype; no production clinical claims.
**Application deadline reported:** 20 October 2026 at 23:59 CAT.

## Root cause to test
Some young Zimbabweans hesitate to seek mental health assistance due to stigma, difficulty opening up to an older professional, lack of trustworthy referral information, costs and uncertainty about what happens next. These are hypotheses: interview prospective participants and qualified local clinicians before making evidence claims.

## Proposed intervention
Zim Safe Minds will test a private, low-data pathway from guided self-expression to user-chosen verified human support. Stage 1 is a non-clinical scripted demonstration. AI-assisted reflective prompts are a *future research feature* conditional on clinical oversight, evaluation and privacy protections; no diagnosis, risk score or autonomous referral. The intended distinction is the accountable, consent-based handoff to appropriate care — not just AI conversation time.

## Pilot outline
Months 1–2: interview youth and clinicians; document barriers and safeguarding requirements.
Months 3–4: ethics/privacy and clinical co-design; choose approved service model.
Months 5–6: verified providers and secure consent-first pathway.
Months 7–8: limited adult-youth pilot (illustrative target 50–100, subject to approvals), 2–3 partners.
Months 9–10: evaluate actual care access, affordability, usability and harms.
Months 11–12: revise, report, and make evidence-based scale/stop decision.

- Accessible platform prototype: $1,200
- Provider verification and onboarding: $700
- Recruitment/outreach and participation support: $1,100

## Evidence
Measure successful connection to verified professional care, referral follow-through (only with consent and real partners), barriers encountered, participant trust and accessibility, and adverse/privacy incidents. Do not present engagement statistics as improved mental health.

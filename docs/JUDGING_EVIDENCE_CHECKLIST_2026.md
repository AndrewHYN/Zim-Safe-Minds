# HackLab 2026 — judge-readiness audit

The official scoring is 100 points (60 for initial shortlisting and 65 for grant eligibility, **not** a guaranteed win). Our application should satisfy all six criteria without fictional claims.

| Criterion | Max points | Present | Missing high-value evidence |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Problem–solution fit | 20 | Exact validated Category 1 stigma barrier; product addresses first contact | Five or more real youth discovery conversations |
| Innovation | 20 | Optional AI reflection, personal control, preference-based intro, independent services | Demonstrate why existing pathways do not fully solve this for pilot users |
| Feasibility | 20 | React demo, $5,000 costs, 12-month milestones, safety gates | Provider costing and clinical adviser |
| Youth leadership | 15 | Youth-created working prototype | Applicant's actual age, roles, optional verified team |
| Impact/scalability | 15 | Contact completion, availability and access metrics; district replication theory | Real pilot baseline, partner commitment |
| Intersectionality | 10 | Gender-sensitive, disability, low-data and language research plan | Accessibility testing and SRHR-informed clinical feedback |

**Important:** Scoring weights are official; readiness column is a qualitative work list, not a claim of earned points.

## Highest-leverage steps before deadline
- Five or more consented, anonymized *nonclinical* youth interviews on how someone finds trustworthy help (not symptoms/diagnoses).
- One conversation with a qualified local mental-health professional about credentialing, crisis boundaries and referral safety; no endorsement implied.
- One real low-end Android walkthrough; document actual problems found and fixes.
- Confirm lead identity, residency, project ownership and genuine team membership.
- Save screenshot or video of the deployed product once verified.

## Judge challenge and grounded response
**“Doesn't Friendship Bench already offer WhatsApp care?”** Yes. It is an independent public service. We propose to test a safer bridge from hesitation to trustworthy first contact, where user choice and consent matter. If research shows no gap, we will pivot rather than duplicate it.

**“Why use AI?”** Optional reflection may lower pressure to start expressing concerns, but the clinical work stays with humans. Current demo is scripted and AI deployment is explicitly safety-gated.

**“Can $5,000 buy an AI therapist?”** No. It funds a limited pilot and safe navigation workflow, not autonomous therapy or a nationwide service.

**“How will impact be measured?”** Actual verified first contacts, drop-offs, time to connect, affordability and safety incidents, with a documented denominator and user consent.

# Safety and clinical boundaries

Zim Safe Minds is currently a **scripted software demo, not clinical care**. The interface must never say it provides AI therapy, medical advice, crisis monitoring or real provider bookings.

## Hard launch gates
- Partner with appropriately registered Zimbabwean mental-health clinicians for clinical oversight.
- Verify providers independently and get their permission before publishing profiles.
- Independently review emergency and safeguarding guidance; no unverified emergency numbers.
- Keep a visible independent urgent-help route; phrase triggers cannot be relied upon as risk detection.
- Conduct data protection, Zimbabwe legal and security assessments before collecting sensitive information.
- Build a consent-first referral process: no transcript forwarding by default.
- Establish risk handling and incident escalation with named responsible humans; don't promise 24/7 monitoring.
- Do not expose real users, minors or health information to unvalidated AI models.
- No real users in a clinical pilot until appropriate approvals are documented.

## Demo privacy

There are no accounts, server chat logs, real clinicians or outbound referral actions. Chat messages reside in React component memory and disappear on refresh. Browser/network metadata may be recorded by a hosting provider and Google Fonts is fetched externally; do not claim complete anonymity.

## Immediate-danger message
Encourage contacting local emergency services or the nearest emergency department. Do not claim rescue is being dispatched. The phrase matcher is illustrative, *not* a medical-risk engine.

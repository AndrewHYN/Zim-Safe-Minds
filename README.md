## In one minute: what are we building?

**Zim Safe Minds is a youth-focused mental wellbeing and care-navigation platform for Zimbabwe.** It aims to make the first step toward mental-health support less intimidating, especially for young people who hesitate to open up because of stigma, fear of judgment or uncertainty about where to find care.

Our proposed product combines:

1. **Talk:** a gentle entry page with optional guided reflection and grounding exercises. **AI is a core planned feature**, but the demo responses are scripted.
2. **Learn:** original awareness posters and six educational, source-linked articles with topic search and filters; not a social feed or live news.
3. **Connect:** learn about accessible human care, choose language/format/affordability, and prepare an optional first-contact or appointment inquiry draft. **No real appointments or referrals occur today**. An approved pilot could enable consent-based introductions to independently verified professionals.

**The problem isn't that Zimbabwe has no mental-health services.** Services such as Friendship Bench already exist. We want to test whether a private, approachable bridge can help young people **actually take the first step to trustworthy human care**. We will not claim that this works until we gather evidence.

The HackLab focus is **one category only**: Category 1. Our primary root cause is **social, cultural and behavioural barriers to seeking health services**, especially stigma.

## See the product before the meeting

**Open:** https://andrewhyn.github.io/Zim-Safe-Minds/

In a 3–4 minute walkthrough, try the **three primary tabs**:

1. **Talk:** a welcoming landing screen, the consent-gated scripted chat, optional starter prompts and a grounding pause.
2. **Learn:** poster cards, topic filters, search, a full awareness article, its independent source link, and session-only saves.
3. **Connect:** the appointment-planning *preview* (choose support format/timing and copy an inquiry), preference-based navigation, and the independent published Friendship Bench resource. **No actual booking or doctor connection is activated.**

Use the theme switch for **Daylight** (medical teal/mint) and **Afterglow** (low-glare navy/sea glass). The three tabs are deliberate: **Talk · Learn · Connect**, with urgent-help guidance always accessible.

**Please do not enter real mental-health histories, diagnoses, phone numbers or other sensitive health information in the demo.** It is not monitored by a therapist or crisis team.

## How we pitch this idea to our own team

> **A lot of young people find it hard to ask for mental-health help.** Zim Safe Minds is our proposed first step: a welcoming space for guided reflection, followed by a simple choice-based way to find real, qualified human support. We already have a working web prototype. The AI assistance is an important part of the future vision, but we won't present our scripted demo as clinical AI. With the HackLab's proposed US$5,000 seed grant, we would research the real access problem, work with clinical advisers, verify provider pathways, and run a controlled pilot. Our goal is not to make users talk to a bot longer—it is to help them **reach human care**.

## Getting started as a developer

React + TypeScript + Vite. The demo is static by design; it currently uses neither an LLM API nor a backend storing health data.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/AndrewHYN/Zim-Safe-Minds.git
cd Zim-Safe-Minds
npm install
npm test
npm run build
npm run dev
```

Open the local URL printed by Vite. Contributions should be small, focused pull requests with test/build results and mobile screenshots where useful. **No production AI, collection of health histories, provider booking or referral endpoints without the clinical, privacy and security approvals** documented in [SAFETY.md](docs/SAFETY.md).

**Our short-term goal:** a reliable, easy-to-explain prototype and a truthful team-led HackLab application—not an unsafe rushed therapy product.

## v0.2 — Three tabs, one clear story

**Talk · Learn · Connect** is the focused product demonstrated for the HackLab pitch. AI-guided reflection remains part of our vision, not a production claim. Learn contains six original editorial awareness posts with source links (not scraped or live news). Connect has both a user-controlled first-contact note and an **appointment inquiry planner**, but no calendar reservation, live clinician messaging, payment or real provider agreement.

**Product walkthrough:** open the [live demo](https://andrewhyn.github.io/Zim-Safe-Minds/) and show the three main tabs in order. Work on real AI and clinician bookings is deliberately deferred until safety and partnership milestones. The research proposition remains reducing *stigma and friction in the first step toward human help*.

**Developer notes:** [Three-tab product scope and acceptance criteria](docs/PRODUCT_THREE_TABS.md) · [Design system](docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md).

**Default appearance:** Afterglow (dark medical navy with mint/sea-glass accents). Visitors can still switch to Daylight from the header; no account required.

## v0.3 — Visible chat on the opening screen

The **working Talk chat is now in the top hero**, replacing the decorative faux conversation. People see real message bubbles, starter suggestions and a strong message composer without scrolling through an entire second section. The high-contrast, frosted sea-glass card deliberately stands out against the dark Afterglow page (and remains distinct in Daylight).

The consent modal remains mandatory before sending messages. The composer launches consent on focus/tap; a chosen starter is held locally and sent only after informed confirmation. No real AI, therapy, remote chat persistence or professional booking was introduced. The old duplicated lower chat layout was removed and replaced with clearly labeled grounding/awareness/human support choices.

## v0.3.1 — Original graphics restored, visible chat beneath

Per team feedback, the floating glowing **original hero illustration** is kept intact (orbit rings, floating chat preview, gentle notes). The **real interactive** high-contrast sea-glass chat follows **directly below the hero graphics**, before the other cards; it is not buried behind repeated text sections. The hero conversation button scrolls to that working chat, and consent still gates all chat actions. Daylight theme remains optional.

## v0.4 — Luminous Embrace visual identity

The generated brand mark (two figures forming an embracing heart and subtle signal line) is used consistently in the header, demo cards and conversation interface. The matching full **Zim Safe Minds** wordmark is shown in the footer on a pale sea-glass inset so its dark-blue lettering remains legible in Afterglow. It is not stretched or recolored. The new symbol is used for the 32-pixel browser favicon and 180-pixel Apple touch icon.

Source assets: `public/brand/zim-safe-minds-mark.webp`, `public/brand/zim-safe-minds-wordmark.webp`, `public/favicon-32.png`, and `public/apple-touch-icon.png`. Assets are built from the approved generated artwork, resized and optimized for web delivery; no third-party logo fonts or image hosts are required. The icon has a quiet glow animation **only in the main header**, disabled for visitors who prefer reduced motion. Asset URLs honor Vite's base path, keeping Vercel and GitHub Pages compatible.

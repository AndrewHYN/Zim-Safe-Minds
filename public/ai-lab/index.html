BEFORE WE TALK / 01

A little care,
before we begin.

This experimental conversation uses OpenAI. What you type is sent to an external AI provider to generate a reply. The prototype doesn't create accounts or deliberately store conversations, but this is not a promise of confidentiality or a substitute for professional care.

  • Do not include full names, exact addresses, health records, or other identifying details.
  • The AI may misunderstand your situation and cannot diagnose or treat mental-health conditions.
  • No doctor, emergency responder, or monitoring team will be contacted automatically.
  • For urgent danger, contact local emergency services or your nearest emergency department now.
